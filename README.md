# Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

##  Project Overview
This project analyzes sales performance from an ecommerce business to identify key revenue drivers, customer buying trends, and product performance. The goal is to help stakeholders understand **which products sell the most**, **when customers buy**, and **which regions need attention**, enabling informed business decisions.

---

##  Objectives
- Analyze total revenue and order trends over time
- Identify best-selling products and categories
- Compare sales across different **regions** and **customer segments**
- Understand seasonal or monthly purchase trends
- Create an **interactive dashboard** for quick decision-making
